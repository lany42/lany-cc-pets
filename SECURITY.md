# Reporting bugs and security issues

Report every bug and every security issue on the issue tracker:
<https://github.com/lany42/lany-cc-pets/issues>. It is the fastest way to
reach the maintainer.

A good report has:

1. **A summary**, as the issue's title: one line saying what goes wrong,
   such as "The pet window stays blank after `/pet window`".
2. **A detailed description**: what you did, what you expected, and what
   happened instead; the steps to make it happen again; your Claude Code
   version (`claude --version`), your operating system, terminal and
   font; and the look you wear (its id, as `/pet as` takes it).
3. **For a security issue, its impact**: what someone could do with it,
   to whom, and under which conditions, such as reading data the pet
   should not see or running code it should not run.

Issues are public. Describe a security issue well enough to fix it, but
leave out working exploit code, tokens, and anything from your sessions
you would not publish.
